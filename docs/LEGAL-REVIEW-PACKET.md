# Legal review packet

> **DRAFT FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW.** This packet is a drafting aid, not legal advice or a representation of compliance.

Before a client deployment is published, counsel should review the client-specific privacy policy, terms, cookie disclosures, SMS/email consent language, payment and recurring-membership terms, gift-card and cancellation rules, accessibility statements, governing law, dispute terms, and applicable state or international requirements.

The client services agreement should separately address platform IP versus client content, license scope, hosting and third-party account ownership, support/SLA, fees, maintenance, termination, data return/deletion, subprocessors, breach response, customer-request assistance, and the client's responsibilities for tax, licensing, insurance, employment, accessibility, and marketing consent.

Do not publish this packet as customer-facing terms. Remove placeholders and record counsel approval outside the application before changing a policy from Draft to Published.
